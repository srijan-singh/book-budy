3 — The chat

The ribbon is the spoiler boundary

The chapter map shows a physical divider — "Buddy's knowledge ends here" — right where progress stops. It's the same filter enforced in the C retrieval layer, made visible so trust in the spoiler-safety isn't just implied, it's shown.

Marginalia, not a generic chat app

Buddy's replies carry a small ribbon tab and an optional citation line, echoing a note left in the margin of a book rather than a typical messaging bubble — reinforcing "companion," not "search bar."

Quick chips carry the three modes

Catch me up, clarify, and just chat map directly to the three interaction modes from the architecture — surfaced as one-tap shortcuts so users don't need to phrase a full question to get going.
